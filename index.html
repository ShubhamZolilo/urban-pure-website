How It Works

What actually happens to it.

A purifier is a stack of stages, and you pay for every one you fit. Knowing what each does is the difference between buying the right machine and buying the biggest one on the shelf.

Not every home needs all five. Newtown gets a mix of treated supply and borewell. Bring a bottle of your tap water and we will read its TDS with you.

Ask for a water check →

01

Sediment

Traps sand, silt and rust from the tank and the pipes.

02

Activated Carbon

Absorbs chlorine, odour and the taste that comes with it.

03

RO Membrane

Removes dissolved salts, hardness and heavy metals.

04

UV

Kills bacteria and viruses on the way out of the tap.

05

Mineral Booster

Puts back calcium and magnesium that RO strips out.
